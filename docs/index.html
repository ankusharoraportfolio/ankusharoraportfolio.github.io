Fierce Guppy Game Labs · 2025 – present

FG Booth Control — one platform, many branded games

Problem. US clients run marketing games at trade-show booths. Each event needs its own branding, rules, timings and lead capture — and the booth has to work even when the venue Wi-Fi doesn't.

What I built. A reusable platform that runs around 10 games from one shared codebase:

  • JSON-driven configuration: gameplay rules, timings, branding, themes and registration change without a new build
  • Booth flow as a configurable state machine — attract, register, play, game over, leaderboard
  • Companion registration tablet with a player queue, auto-discovered over the local network
  • PlayFab leaderboards and player data, with an offline mode and CSV lead export
  • Licence management to control deployments per client

Result. New branded games ship by configuring and skinning the platform instead of rebuilding the booth stack for every event.
